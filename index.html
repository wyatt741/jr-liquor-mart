Old Town local since 1997

JR Liquor Mart has held down the same corner of E Ventura Blvd since 1997.

The store got a full remodel a few years back, and customers noticed: clean, organized, stocked deep and always cold. The bourbon wall and the walk-in Beer Cave are the two corners people talk about most, and the counter treats regulars like friends.

Next door is JR Smoke Zone, our sister shop. Between the two, this corner of Old Town has you covered.
